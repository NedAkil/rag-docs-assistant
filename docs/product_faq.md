# Lumen Dashboard Product FAQ

> Fictional document for demo purposes. "Lumen Dashboard" is a made-up product.

## What is Lumen Dashboard?
Lumen is a self-serve business intelligence tool that connects to your database and builds charts without writing SQL.

## Which plans are available?
- **Starter**: 19 USD per user per month, up to 5 users, 3 dashboards.
- **Team**: 49 USD per user per month, unlimited dashboards, scheduled email reports.
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing, SSO (SAML), audit logs and a 99.95% uptime SLA.

## Which data sources are supported?
PostgreSQL, MySQL, BigQuery, Snowflake and CSV upload. Connectors for MongoDB are on the roadmap but not yet available.

## Is there a free trial?
Yes. All plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required.

## How often does data refresh?
Starter refreshes every 6 hours. Team refreshes every 15 minutes. Enterprise supports near-real-time refresh down to 1 minute.

## Where is my data stored?
Lumen queries your database directly and caches results for up to 24 hours in the EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia) region, chosen at signup. Cached data is encrypted at rest with AES-256.

## How do I contact support?
Email support@lumen.example. Starter gets replies within 2 business days, Team within 8 hours, and Enterprise has a 1-hour response target, 24/7.
