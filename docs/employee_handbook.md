# Northwind Analytics Employee Handbook

> Fictional document for demo purposes. Northwind Analytics is not a real company.

## Working Hours and Remote Work
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the department head. Office days are Tuesday and Thursday for teams based in Toronto.

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 20 vacation days per year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year; the rest expire on March 31. Sick leave is separate: 10 paid sick days per year, no doctor's note required for absences under 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave
Northwind tops up government parental benefits to 100% of base salary for the first 16 weeks for birthing parents and 8 weeks for non-birthing parents.

## Learning Budget
Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 CAD for courses, books and conferences. Requests above this amount need VP approval. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Expenses
Submit expenses in the Expensify portal within 30 days. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 60 CAD per day. Flights over 800 CAD must be pre-approved by a manager.

## Equipment
New hires receive a laptop and a 300 CAD home-office stipend. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.
